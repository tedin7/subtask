# Governance

subtask has a single maintainer, [@tedin7](https://github.com/tedin7), who has final say.

- Decisions happen in public issues and pull requests. Open an issue before large changes.
- Small fixes can go straight to a pull request.
- Contributors who land several good changes may be invited to help review.
- Releases are cut by the maintainer, following [RELEASING.md](RELEASING.md).
- Conduct is covered by [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md); security by [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
